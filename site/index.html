Why "not carried" is not "wrong"

An outlet can leave a fact out because it judged it unimportant, because it ran a shorter piece, or because it filed earlier. None of those is an error.

The gap is worth seeing anyway: it is the difference between the story you got and the story that was available.

What this does not do

It does not detect contradictions. Across 151 story groups it found none between two different outlets, so there is no count for them.

It does not label an outlet supportive or critical, score accuracy, or rank outlets by trust. It does not decide which of two numbers is right.

Where two outlets publish figures too far apart to be one quantity, both are shown with their sentences and neither is called wrong.
